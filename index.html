Part 2 · Oral assessment (20 points)

Prepare a 2–3 minute presentation in English. Your teacher will assess it in person. Do not submit a written paragraph.

  1. Describe a device you use regularly. What do you use it for? (Present Simple + purpose infinitive)
  2. Explain what you or your classmates are working on these days. (Present Continuous)
  3. Describe a technology problem you solved in the past. (Past Simple)
  4. Compare two devices and explain which is the best for a student. (Comparatives + superlatives)
  5. Explain one skill you want to develop for your future in computing.

Oral rubric: Grammar 6 · Technical vocabulary 4 · Fluency 4 · Pronunciation 3 · Content and clarity 3.

Your final mark will be confirmed by your teacher after the oral assessment.
